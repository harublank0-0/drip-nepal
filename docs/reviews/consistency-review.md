# Consistency review

Status: Complete (2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04)

This record describes the consistency review of the documentation set: what it checked, how it ran, the decisions made along the way, the conventions the documents now share, the state of the checks at the end, and what is still open. The review came after all documents were written and after the deep audits of [05](../05-order-payment-and-inventory-lifecycles.md) (audit 1), [04 and 04a](../04-domain-model-and-data-dictionary.md) (audit 2) and [07](../07-security-threat-model-and-permissions.md) (audit 4). Its goal was a self-contained set in which every fact has one owning document and every other document agrees with it, so that coding can start from the documents alone.

## 1. What was checked

- **Self-containment.** No document may rely on material outside this repository: planning notes, local file paths or earlier research summaries. Each such reference was replaced with a link to the document that now owns the fact, or to [docs/research](../research/README.md).
- **Follow-ups from the audits and from documents 10 and 12.** Every item the audits and the writing of [10](../10-testing-and-quality-gates.md) and [12](../12-roadmap-and-backlog.md) handed to other documents was applied or recorded as open.
- **Agreement with the owner.** Where two documents state the same fact (a name, value, status code, error code, permission, state, transition, column, milestone or decision status), they must agree, and the owning document wins. The owners are listed in [00 §1.3](../00-context-assumptions-and-questions.md#13-id-schemes) and in each document's reading guide.
- **Contract parity.** The operations of [06 §13](../06-api-design.md#13-endpoint-catalogue) and [openapi.yaml](../openapi.yaml) must match one for one.

## 4. Conventions the documents now share

- **The owner wins.** A document that restates a rule links to its owner; when the two differ, the owner is right until a decision changes it.
- **Labels.** [Assumption], [Open OD-xx] and [Verify-external VX-xx] follow [00 §1.2](../00-context-assumptions-and-questions.md#12-evidence-labels). Assumed values are registered as A-01 to A-52 in [00 §6](../00-context-assumptions-and-questions.md#6-proposed-defaults-and-assumptions-register).
- **Test IDs.** Every test ID is in the [10 §6](../10-testing-and-quality-gates.md#6-test-id-registry) registry. The base IDs are unmarked or written "base (10 §6)"; the others keep their "(proposed)" mark where they are cited.
- **Proposed names.** An operation, error code, column or audit action that is not yet approved keeps its "(proposed)" mark until the product owner approves it or the change that implements it lands. The proposed operations are listed in [06 §13.8](../06-api-design.md#138-proposed-operations) and in the `x-pending-operations` list of openapi.yaml.
- **ADRs.** Accepted ADRs keep their decisions. Before the first production release a correction is a dated "Edited YYYY-MM-DD" note next to the text it changes; after it, a superseding ADR.
- **Consistency notes.** Each document ends with notes that record what changed and what is still open. A closed item reads "Done in the consistency review, 2026-09-30: …", the label date of this review.
- **Header.** Each document has one "Reviewed:" line with its review, audit and consistency-review dates.
