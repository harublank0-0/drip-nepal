# Seller agreement

## This is a draft

This page is a placeholder, not the seller agreement. DripNepal is being built, and the final agreement will replace this draft before any seller other than DripNepal's own shop joins.

Until then, nothing on this page is an agreement, a promise or legal advice.

## What the final agreement will cover

- Who may sell on DripNepal, and the documents a seller provides.
- The commission and how it is calculated.
- How cash collected on delivery is settled with DripNepal.
- Who invoices the buyer, and how tax is shown.
- Accurate listings, genuine goods, and what may not be sold.
- Delivery, returns and refunds, and how complaints are handled.
- How the agreement changes, and how sellers are told.
