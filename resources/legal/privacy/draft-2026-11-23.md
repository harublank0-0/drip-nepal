# Privacy notice

## This is a draft

This page is a placeholder, not the privacy notice. DripNepal is being built, and the final privacy notice will replace this draft before DripNepal opens to the public.

Until then, nothing on this page is a promise or legal advice.

## What the final notice will cover

- What personal information DripNepal collects when you create an account, place an order or contact support.
- Why it is used, and who it is shared with, such as the shop that sells you an item and the courier that delivers it.
- How long it is kept.
- How you can see, correct or delete your information, and how to stop marketing messages.
- How to raise a concern about your information.

## Questions

If you have a question about this draft, contact support using the details on this site.
