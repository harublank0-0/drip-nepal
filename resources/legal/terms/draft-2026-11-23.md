# Terms of use

## This is a draft

This page is a placeholder, not the terms of use. DripNepal is being built, and its terms are being written with legal advice. The final terms will replace this draft before DripNepal opens to the public.

Until then, nothing on this page is an agreement, a promise or legal advice.

## What the final terms will cover

- Who may create an account, and how an account is kept safe.
- How orders work on a marketplace where each shop sells and ships its own products.
- Cash on delivery, delivery, and what happens when a parcel cannot be delivered.
- Returns, refunds and how to raise a complaint.
- What is not allowed on DripNepal.
- How the terms change, and how you will be told.
