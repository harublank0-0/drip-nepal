It is a framework-neutral guide to integrating Nepal Can Move. In DripNepal it does not decide anything:

- **Its NCM API facts are not all verified.** It labels facts [doc], [observed], [observed 2026-11-16], [reported], [inferred] or [unknown] after NCM documents, integrations and read-only production calls that are not in this repository. A fact labelled [observed 2026-11-16] is what NCM's production API returned to one read-only call; NCM has not confirmed it. They do not close VX-16 (`docs/risks-and-open-decisions.md` §3.1), and decision 6 of ADR-0019 (the `CourierProvider` port) stays Proposed until VX-16 closes.
- **Where it disagrees with the documents, the documents win.** Shipment states and their transitions are in docs/05 §6.3, the tables in docs/04a §9.8 to §9.14 and §11, money in integer paisa (ADR-0007), the courier port in ADR-0019, the open COD question for platform-owned accounts in OD-58. Its "decisions to settle with the user" are settled in those documents or registered as open decisions there. The cross-NCM customer rating is used by no document: building on it would first need an open decision in the risks register, under the personal-data rules of docs/07 §5.
