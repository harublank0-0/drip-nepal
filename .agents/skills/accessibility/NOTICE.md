This is the `accessibility` skill of [`addyosmani/web-quality-skills`](https://github.com/addyosmani/web-quality-skills), copied verbatim on 2026-11-26 from its folder `skills/accessibility/` at commit `afa8da942115f2961fdbfa80807ea0b232ff6c00`. `skills-lock.json` at the repository root records the source and a hash of the copied files. Its `SKILL.md` frontmatter declares `license: MIT`, and `LICENSE` in this folder is a copy of the upstream repository's MIT licence (copyright 2026 Addy Osmani). It has no scripts, runs nothing when it loads, pre-approves no tool and fetches no remote instructions. The link at the end of `SKILL.md` to `../web-quality-audit/SKILL.md` points to an upstream skill that is not copied here. The same repository's `performance`, `core-web-vitals` and `seo` skills were read and not copied, because most of their advice is already decided, often differently, in docs/08 §10, docs/03 §12.5 and FR-SRCH-005.

It is a WCAG 2.2 audit checklist. DripNepal's target is WCAG 2.2 AA (docs/08 §9), and the skill tracks it closely, so it is a good review aid for storefront and seller pages. It decides nothing: **where it disagrees with the documents, the documents win.** The differences known when it was copied:

- **Large-text contrast is wrong in the skill.** `SKILL.md` gives 3:1 for text of at least 18 px, or 14 px bold, which confuses points with pixels. The threshold is text of at least 24 px, or 18.66 px bold (docs/08 §5.10, `T-UI-003`); below that, text needs 4.5:1.
- **Focus indicator**: a 2 px `--ring` outline with a 2 px offset, and the `focus-visible:border-ring` plus `ring-ring/50` halo that the `ui/` primitives keep (docs/08 §9.1, §5.12). Not the skill's `currentColor` outline or brand box-shadow.
- **Focus not obscured**: `scroll-padding-top` and `scroll-padding-bottom` on the scrolling element, from `--header-h` and `--bottom-bar-h` (docs/08 §6.1 rule 4). Not the skill's fixed `scroll-margin` values.
- **Dialogs and sheets** are the Radix primitives of shadcn/ui, which trap focus and return it to the trigger (docs/08 §9.1, ADR-0015). Not a hand-written focus trap or a native `<dialog>`.
- **Form errors** follow docs/08 §7.1, §7.4 and §11.7: the error summary takes focus when there are two or more errors or the form is long, a single error on a short form moves focus to its field, and focus and announcement are never both used. Live announcements go through the two root live regions and `announce()`; assertive ones are only for session expiry and `RATE_LIMITED`. Not the skill's `role="alert"` on each field error.
- **Session expiry** saves a draft to `sessionStorage`, sends the person to `/login` and restores the draft afterwards (docs/08 §7.1); idle limits are enforced by the server per surface (docs/07 §3.4). Not a warning modal with "Extend session".
- **Accessible authentication** (SC 3.3.8): pasting allowed, password managers supported, one TOTP field (docs/08 §9.3). Passkeys and email login links are not scheduled; passkeys are an R2 revisit (ADR-0005).
- **Hidden text and skip links**: Tailwind's `sr-only`, `<main id="main" tabindex="-1">`, and colours from the design tokens (docs/08 §9.1, §9.3, §5). Not a custom `.visually-hidden` class or hard-coded colours.
- **How it is verified**: axe in Japa's browser suite (`T-A11Y-001`, docs/10) and the manual release checklist with a screen reader (docs/08 §9.4), with pages checked through the `dripnepal-run` skill. Not `npx lighthouse` or a global `npm install @axe-core/cli`: this repository uses pnpm, and installs and browser runs follow the project's own commands.

Its agreements with the documents include the 24 px minimum and 44 px comfortable target sizes (docs/08 §6.4), the reduced-motion rule (docs/08 §5.7), consistent help placement, `<html lang="en">`, and a manual pass because automated tools catch only part of the problems (docs/08 §9.4).
